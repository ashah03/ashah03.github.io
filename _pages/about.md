I recently graduated from UC Berkeley with a degree in Electrical Engineering and Computer Sciences. I'm currently a researcher at the AlQuraishi Lab at Columbia, where I'm working on problems at the intersection of machine learning and structural biology.

I was previously the first computational hire at Until Labs, where I did all things software and machine learning towards the goal of reversible cryopreservation of biological systems. Before that, I worked at the Broad Institute on AI for extracting information from high-throughput images of cells for drug discovery.

I've also worked on machine learning methods for brain-to-speech interfaces for paralyzed patients, as well as on using semi-autonomous vehicles to mitigate traffic congestion and reduce carbon emissions.

Lately I've been thinking about [biophysics](https://www.rpgroup.caltech.edu/pboc2/), [orders of magnitude](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0fKBhvDjuy0), and [urbanism](https://www.astralcodexten.com/p/your-book-review-progress-and-poverty). Feel free to reach out!
